03 / The Jarmai House Standard

Precision Needlework,
Tailored for Distinction.

Every Jarmai Fashion Design creation begins with exacting measurements and hand-selected cloth—rich cashmere-blend suiting for royal Agbada capes, crisp Egyptian cotton for honeycomb Senator tunics, and vibrant statement Ankara prints structured for modern womenswear.

Our master tailors reinforce collars, mitre every chest pocket, and finish plackets with interlocking diamond and medallion threadwork so each garment drapes effortlessly and holds its architectural poise for years.
