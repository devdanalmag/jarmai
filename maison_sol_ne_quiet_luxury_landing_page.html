03 / The Jarmai House Standard

Precision Needlework,
Tailored for Distinction.

At Jarmai Fashion Design, tailoring is an art of millimeters. From the hand-guided mandala embroidery on our sovereign Agbadas to razor-sharp geometric plackets on crisp Egyptian cotton kaftans, every garment is drafted individually to each client's posture and stride.

Our master tailors at Green Garden Complex reinforce collars, mitre chest pocket borders, and balance fabric drape so that your garment holds its majestic form through long ceremonies and celebrations.
